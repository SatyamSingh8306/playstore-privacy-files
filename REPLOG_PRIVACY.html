RepLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

RepLog is a local-first workout logger. Your training data stays on your device. We do not run an account system, a server, or analytics, and we do not send your workout data anywhere.

What RepLog stores on your device

When you use the app, the following information is saved locally in an on-device SQLite database:

  • Profile: name, age, height, weight, preferred units, fitness goal, experience level, and training days per week.
  • Workouts: exercises, sets, weight, reps, RPE, warm-up flags, timestamps, duration, volume, and notes.
  • Templates and splits: workout templates you create or use, and your weekly training split.
  • Measurements: body-weight and other measurement entries you choose to log.
  • Saved programs: which programs you bookmark in the app.

This data exists only in the app's local storage on your device. RepLog does not upload it, sync it, or share it with us or anyone else.

What RepLog does not collect

  • No account, email address, or password.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising identifiers or ad SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No automatic transmission of your workouts over the internet.

The exercise library and demonstration images are bundled inside the app, so browsing exercises does not require a network connection.

Third parties

RepLog does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection SDKs. The app is built with Expo and React Native; those frameworks provide the runtime but are not configured here to collect personal data.

Children

RepLog is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the address below.

Your choices and data removal

You can edit or delete your profile and workout data inside the app. Uninstalling RepLog removes all locally stored data from your device. Because there is no cloud backup, deleted data cannot be recovered by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
